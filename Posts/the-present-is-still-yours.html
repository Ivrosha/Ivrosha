21 November 2026·Moving forward

The present is still yours.

I wish I could go back in time.

I want to correct all those mistakes.

But here I am, in the present, still unable to do so.

I need to correct what's happening now, but somehow, I still keep getting stuck.

But...

Please come out of your past.

Yeah, you could have made things different back then, but you can still make your future different if you focus on your present. 🌱

You cannot change what has already happened, but you still have a chance to change what happens next. 🤍

~Ivrosha
