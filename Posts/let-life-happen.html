24 November 2026·Reflections

Let life happen. Keep going.

I had been feeling a bit stuck and lost for the last few days. I had been doing nothing, just scrolling and sleeping. I was thinking about doing so many things but was unable to get myself to do any of them.

Today, I just started with the hope that I would do something better. I didn't know what exactly I should do, so I just opened YouTube to study something. I was randomly trying to study different things. I watched a few lectures on AI and ML. For the last few days, I had also been watching, The Summer Story, The Winter Story and The monsoon story web series, but after completing three seasons, I had stopped watching them. Then, this episode popped up on my YouTube feed from the Summer and Rachel story: What You Are Seeking Is Seeking You! After watching a few lectures, I somehow ended up watching that episode. The series was talking about unexpected surprises, and somehow, today turned out to be a good day. I was finally feeling better. In the evening, I thought of going for a walk. I went alone and was in a good mood. There's a friend of mine whom I haven't met in a few days. And then, he came out of nowhere! We ended up going for a walk together. The views were beautiful, and the evening turned out to be even better than I had expected.

Maybe it's not always about finding the answers you're looking for. Sometimes, when you feel stuck, just surrender to the universe and keep going. 🤍

~Ivrosha
